Offshore wind farm begins powering 400,000 homes along the coast

The 62-turbine array came online three months ahead of schedule, and engineers say a second phase is already in planning.

Meera Joshi · 6 min read · Oct 15, 2026
The array sits 22 km offshore, beyond the visible horizon from most beaches. Illustration: Northline Graphics

When the last of the 62 turbines spun up on Tuesday morning, the control room broke into applause. The array now feeds enough electricity into the regional grid to power roughly 400,000 homes.

“We were told the weather window alone would cost us a season,” said the project’s lead engineer.
